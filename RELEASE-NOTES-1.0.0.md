# PC Tweaker 1.0.0 — "Control Room"

**The biggest release in PC Tweaker's history.** After fifty verified tweaks, a signed licensing system, and months of obsessive polish, PC Tweaker graduates to 1.0 — and it arrives as something no other Windows tuner is: a control room, not a checkbox list.

## What's new

### 🧠 PC Tweaker Intelligence
Your PC finally gets an advisor that argues from evidence.

- **Recommended for your PC** — one concrete suggestion on the home screen, chosen from your actual hardware: your disk type, your form factor, your GPU. It tells you *why* it applies to your machine, how confident it is, and reminds you of the promise everything here is built on: **the original value is saved before any change**. It never applies anything by itself, and it is structurally incapable of suggesting security-reducing tweaks — the allowlist that feeds it excludes them by construction, and a unit test proves it.
- **Change Ledger** — a new History screen: every change this app has ever made to your PC, newest first, with one-click revert for anything still applied. Stored locally. Never uploaded. Your machine, your record.

### 🎛 The Control Room design language
An interface built for this app alone — no template, no borrowed kit. A three-layer token system derives the entire UI from three raw values per theme, which is how **14 themes** stay perfectly coherent. Live signal hairlines, tabular figures where numbers matter, motion that respects your reduced-motion settings, and over **320 hardcoded colors retired** in one release.

### ⚡ A faster, calmer app
- **Free RAM leads the home screen** — live memory figures, a session trace where every trim shows up as a visible dip, and the automatic cleanup scheduler.
- **Micro-freezes eliminated.** Forty-four internal commands moved off the main thread; scrolling and clicking never stall again, even mid-scan.
- **An instrument-grade Turbo Boost gauge** — dial numerals, a tapered needle with a counterweight, a glow channel. It reads like the rev counter it always wanted to be.
- **A theme-aware Scan** — the diagnostic orb and progress arc now wear your theme's accent.

### 👤 Yours, visibly
Set a **profile photo** straight from your device — cover-cropped and stored *only* on your machine (there is no upload endpoint, on purpose). **Report an issue** now lives one click away in the account menu.

### 🧩 A suite, not an app
PC Tweaker now travels with family: **[PC Tweaker Uninstaller](https://github.com/AurelioAvila/pc-tweaker-uninstaller)** (Removal Intelligence for Windows, released today alongside this) and **[PromptShield](https://promptshield-beta.vercel.app)** (catch personal data before a prompt reaches an AI). One account across all of them — and PC Tweaker Pro subscribers unlock Uninstaller Pro at a loyalty price.

## The contract, restated at 1.0

Honest descriptions. A backup before every change. One-click rollback. No fake benchmarks, no security tweaks hiding in the Quick Scan, and now a written, auditable record of everything. That is what 1.0 means here.
