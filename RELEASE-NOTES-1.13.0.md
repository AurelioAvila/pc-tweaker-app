PC Tweaker 1.13.0 introduces a Lifetime campaign price of €79.99 against the €119 list price, shown only while a campaign window is open. The reduction is drawn only when the list price is genuinely higher than the amount charged at checkout.

The campaign banner and its countdown are considerably smaller, so the three plans remain the first thing on screen. The long availability notice is no longer repeated inside the banner; it stays in the pricing terms. Lifetime campaigns are described as rare rather than recurring, and early preview access to new features is listed among the Lifetime benefits.

Windows application and installer signatures are verified before distribution. Code signing identifies the publisher; Windows may still show reputation warnings.
