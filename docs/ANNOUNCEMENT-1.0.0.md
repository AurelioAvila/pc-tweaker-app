## Reddit (r/software, r/Windows11 — text post)

**Title:** PC Tweaker 1.0 "Control Room" — a Windows tuner that keeps a
signed, auditable record of every change it makes

**Body:** After months of building in public, 1.0 is out. The idea has
stayed the same since day one: tweaks with honest descriptions, a backup
of the original value before every change, and one-click rollback. 1.0
adds the two features that idea was always pointing at: an Advisor that
recommends only what your specific hardware justifies (disk type, form
factor, GPU — and it structurally can't suggest security-reducing
tweaks), and a Change Ledger — a local history of everything the app ever
did to your machine, revertible line by line. No fake benchmarks, no
padded "issues found" counters. Free, with an optional Pro tier.
Feedback very welcome.
