PC Tweaker 1.18.12 makes the 3-day free trial easy to find and fixes the plan labels.

- Plans & pricing offers "Start your 3-day free trial" when your account can still start one, counts down the days while it runs and says so once it has been used; locked Pro tools lead with the trial too.
- Plans below the one you hold now read "Included in your plan": Free under Pro, Free and Pro under Lifetime.
- A licence file copied from another PC no longer unlocks Pro; signing in on each PC works as before, with no new device limit.
- The catalog is unchanged: 81 controls, 46 Free and 35 Pro.

Windows binaries and installers are digitally signed by Aurelio Avila and timestamped; automatic updates carry a separate updater signature.
