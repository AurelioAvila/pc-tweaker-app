PC Tweaker 1.18.2 keeps Pro working when this PC's clock runs behind, explains account errors in your language, shows the plan each product has on your account and polishes checkboxes, focus rings and sidebar icons.

- Pro no longer switches off when this PC's clock runs behind. A license signed moments earlier was refused if the clock was more than five minutes behind the account service, which can happen when the time zone is set by hand. The allowance is now one day, the same as the account service's. How long Pro keeps working offline is unchanged: it is still counted from the moment the license was signed.
- Sign-in, registration, verification and password reset now explain what went wrong in the app's language: an incorrect email or password, an email that already has an account (perhaps from PC Tweaker Uninstaller or Tweaky Driver), too many attempts, an account service that is not responding, or no internet connection. Some of these used to show the server's English text or a bare error code. A password longer than 72 bytes is caught before anything is sent.
- The account menu lists PC Tweaker and PC Tweaker Uninstaller with the plan each one has on your account: Lifetime, Annual or Monthly with the date it runs until, Included with Lifetime, or Not active.
- Checkboxes follow the theme in Debloat, Maintenance and Account, with a visible keyboard focus and a clear disabled state. Text fields draw a single focus ring instead of two, every sidebar icon sits in the same space so the labels line up, and the Most chosen label on the Lifetime plan sits on the card's top edge.
- Portuguese: a doubled word is gone from the Hardware screen. Debloat counts use the singular for one app in Italian, French, Spanish and Portuguese.
- The catalog is unchanged: 81 controls, 46 Free and 35 Pro.

Windows binaries and installers are digitally signed by Aurelio Avila and timestamped; automatic updates carry a separate updater signature.
