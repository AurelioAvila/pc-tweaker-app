PC Tweaker 1.18.3 lets you change your password and email from the account menu, sends account emails in the app's language and leaves a running game alone when Pro ends.

- Password and email, in the account menu: change your password (the current one is required; your other PCs are signed out and this one stays signed in) or your email address (the change takes effect when you confirm the link sent to the new address).
- Verification, password reset and other account emails arrive in the language the app is set to.
- If a subscription payment fails, the account menu says so and offers Update payment method, which opens Stripe's billing portal. When Pro stops, the app tells you once, the next time you open it: everything you applied stays in place, and Restore can still undo it.
- When Pro ends while a game is running, Game Sessions, core steering, monitor profiles and app priority rules no longer undo their changes under the game. They start nothing new and finish when the game or app closes.
- The catalog is unchanged: 81 controls, 46 Free and 35 Pro.

Windows binaries and installers are digitally signed by Aurelio Avila and timestamped; automatic updates carry a separate updater signature.
