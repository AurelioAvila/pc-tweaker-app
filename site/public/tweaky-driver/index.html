Before you download

Which Windows versions are supported?

The application targets Windows 10 and Windows 11 on x64 systems. Check the latest release notes for current requirements and known limitations.

How much of my PC does a scan cover?

The scan page tells you: how many devices have a driver from an official source, how many have no reliable package, how many could not be fully checked and how many are handled by Windows itself. Coverage depends on what manufacturers publish through the supported sources, so some devices may not get an official match.

Can I update while other apps are open?

Yes. From version 0.1.18, setup closes only Tweaky Driver and reopens it when the update is done; PC Tweaker, the Uninstaller and your other programs stay open. It never interrupts a driver installation in progress, and it completes an earlier update that did not finish.

Where is Tweaky Driver installed?

From version 0.1.20, in C:\Program Files\Tweaky Driver, as Tweaky Driver.exe. Earlier versions used C:\Program Files\Driver Companion. The first update moves the app to the new folder by itself and points the Start menu entry, your desktop shortcut, a taskbar pin and scheduled scans at it. Settings, downloads and your account stay as they are. New installations offer to start the app and to create a desktop shortcut on the last setup page.

I have Pro or Lifetime. Why does the app show Free?

Update to version 0.1.19 or later and sign in on the Account tab with the email you used to buy. The app checks your plan when it opens, when you come back to its window and right after you sign in. If this PC is not activated yet and one of your two places is free, it activates it automatically; if both places are in use, release one in Plans & Pricing first. If the check cannot finish because you are offline or the account service cannot be reached, the app says so instead of asking you to buy again; from version 0.1.30 it keeps showing your last known plan meanwhile and tries again on its own. From version 0.1.22, a PC clock that runs ahead of the correct time (by minutes or up to a day) no longer makes the app treat a fresh Pro check as expired. Still stuck? Email tweakydriver@pctweaker.app.

How can I verify a download?

Download from the official GitHub release. In Windows file properties, check that the digital signature is valid and identifies Aurelio Avila. Compare the SHA-256 with the official asset digest when available. Code signing does not guarantee that SmartScreen will never display a warning.

Is this an open-source application?

No. Tweaky Driver is proprietary. Its public repository contains distribution information and release notes; the application source is private. Third-party components retain their own licenses.

How does the free scan work?

On the Free plan you get one complete scan every 10 days. Once it has run, you can download and install the drivers it found until the next free scan unlocks; the app shows the countdown on the scan page. The count is kept by the Tweaky Driver service for your account and this PC, so it needs a free account with a verified email; one account works in PC Tweaker, the Uninstaller and Tweaky Driver. Pro, and the 3-day free trial, scan and download without limits.

See Plans above. You subscribe from the app's plans screen, where the final amount and renewal terms are shown before you confirm. This website does not collect payments.

How do I report a problem?

Use Report an issue in the application or email tweakydriver@pctweaker.app. Include your app version, Windows version and reproduction steps, but never passwords or license tokens.
